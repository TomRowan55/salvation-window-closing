Why This Ministry Exists

Many people believe they have waited too long or sinned too much to return to God. This ministry points people to the grace of Jesus Christ while emphasizing the urgency of responding today.

Why The Name Salvation Window Closing?

God's offer of salvation is available today, but Scripture warns against delaying our response. Every person has a limited opportunity to come to Christ.
