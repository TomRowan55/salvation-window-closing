Why This Ministry Exists

Many people believe they have waited too long or sinned too much to return to God. This ministry points people to the grace of Jesus Christ while emphasizing the urgency of responding today.

Why The Name Salvation Window Closing?

God's offer of salvation is available today, but Scripture warns against delaying our response. Every person has a limited opportunity to come to Christ. Prophetic events written in scriptures are not only being fulfilled today but they are doing so at a rapid pace. The stage is being set for the final seven years of mankind's dominion on this earth; unprecedented plagues and evil will reach their pinnacles during this time of God's wrath pouring out upon the unbelievers.

Prophecy Watch

Observing current events through the lens of Scripture and encouraging believers to remain watchful and prepared. In the Apostle Paul's first letter to the Thessalonians, Chapter 4, he details the moment that the believing church of Jesus Christ is taken out off of this earth, human bodies transformed into their indestructible glorified bodies and taken up to meet Jesus in the air where they will be taken into the presence of God the Father.

Have You Trusted Christ?

All of the end time signs pointing to the last seven years, the Great Tribulation, are coming together before our very eyes. Don't Be Left Behind!
