The Gospel of Jesus Christ

God's Good News for a Lost World

What Is the Gospel?

The word "gospel" means "good news." The good news is that God has provided a way for sinful people to be forgiven, reconciled to Him, and receive eternal life through Jesus Christ.

The Problem

Every person has sinned against God.

"For all have sinned, and fall short of the glory of God."
— Romans 3:23

Sin separates us from God and brings spiritual death. No amount of good works, religion, or personal effort can remove that sin.

God's Love

Although we are sinners, God loves us.

"But God commends his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us."
— Romans 5:8

Jesus Paid the Price

Jesus Christ, the Son of God, lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, was buried, and rose from the dead on the third day.

"Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; and that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures."
— 1 Corinthians 15:3-4

How to Be Saved

Salvation is a gift from God received through faith in Jesus Christ.

"For by grace you are saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: not of works, so no one can boast."
— Ephesians 2:8-9

...because, if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved. - Romans 10:9

p> 

>For with the heart one believes and is justified, and with the mouth one confesses and is saved. - Romans 10:10

 

Turn to Christ in faith. Get alone with God, confess your sins the best you can; if you believe what the Bible says about Jesus tell the Father that you believe. Tell him that you know you are a sinner and deserve his punishment. Then thank him for placing that punishment on our willing Savior so that you would be acceptable in his presence...something you could never do on your own merits or good works.

Then ask him to change you from the inside out, a transformation that will be accomplished by the Holy Spirit who indwells you at your moment of true belief in Jesus. Put knowing God at the top of your to-do list and strive to live a life that is pleasing to him. This is a life-long process, not an instant experience. The more earnestly you seek him through prayer and reading of his Word the faster you will grow spiritually in your walk with Jesus.

We have a section on this website where you can encourage others by sharing your testimony of coming to Jesus. Feel free to share your conversion experience and the way Jesus has dramatically changed your life. All submissions will be reviewed before publishing so it might take a day or two for you to see your testimony on the Testimonial page.

May the peace of God which surpasses all understanding be yours in Christ Jesus!
