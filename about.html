The truth

  4. Your sins and mine were laid upon him as he was nailed to a wooden cross. When he cried out, "It is finished!", he was proclaiming that He and the Father's plan for the salvation of mankind was complete. From that moment on all who would put their faith in Jesus Christ would be "born again" and receive the Holy Spirit who would indwell them and transform them over time into the image of Jesus Christ.

  6. The Apostles witness Jesus ascend into Heaven. His final words to them were: "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age.” -Matthew 28:19-20
