It's Never Too Late For Grace...Until It Is.

Our Mission

To alert the unsuspecting public of the soon return of Jesus Christ. The prophecies revealed in the book of Daniel, Zechariah, Revelation, and other prophetic passages are not only coming to life now but they are all starting to converge at an unpresidented rapid pace. The stage is being set for the final and most painful seven years in human history - the outpouring of God's wrath on the unbelieving world...otherwise known as the Great Tribulation. At its conclusion Jesus Christ will return to Earth as the sole King and Ruler of the entire globe.

We want to help you find God's grace through Jesus Christ while you still have time to escape the horror that is coming upon mankind for its rejection of God's free gift of forgiveness and salvation.

No matter what bad things you have done, and we are all guilty sin, God is willing to forgive you if you accept Jesus Christ and his death on the cross and his resurrection from the dead three days later as truth and that you believe he took your sins upon himself so you could be united with God the Father forever as his child...cleansed of all sin and guilt. It is a free gift but, like any gift, you must receive it as your own and show gratitute to the Giver for the great cost of Jesus' self-sacrificing love for you. You show that by seeking him with your whole heart.

To learn how to be saved and be "Born Again" with a new identity as a child of God, click on or The Gospel link in the top menu bar. The Almighty's window of grace will soon close. Today is the day of salvation!
